This Privacy Policy describes how Ethnix Group LLC, a Motion Logistics company ("Ethnix Group", "we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects personal information in connection with the MyEthnix mobile application (the "App"). The App is a warehouse management tool intended exclusively for Ethnix Group employees and authorized business partners, and requires a company-issued account.

1.Information We Collect

We collect only what our warehouse and logistics workflows require:

  • Account information: your name, username, and role within the organization, as registered in our warehouse management system.
  • Authentication data: the credentials you use to sign in. Passwords are transmitted securely and never stored in plain text on the device.
  • Operational content: photos you capture to document truck loading and related warehouse operations, along with the barcodes and records you scan or process.
  • Activity records: actions performed in the App (route assignments, pallet movements, label printing, authorizations), associated with your account for operational traceability.

The App does not collect your precise location, contacts, health data, financial information, or browsing history.

2.How We Use Information

Information collected through the App is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you and apply role-based permissions;
  • Operate warehouse workflows such as routing, staging, pallet consolidation, truck loading, and label printing;
  • Maintain operational and audit records of warehouse activity;
  • Provide technical support and keep our systems secure.

3.Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, each tied to a specific feature:

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and photograph truck loading.
  • Photo library: to attach or save photos related to loading documentation.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to thermal label printers in the warehouse.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time; the related feature will stop working, but the rest of the App remains usable.

4.Data Sharing

Personal information is processed within Ethnix Group systems and by service providers that host our infrastructure under contractual confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Ethnix Group, its employees, or partners. We never share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5.Data Security and Retention

Data travels between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access to operational data is restricted by role-based permissions. We retain operational records for as long as needed to meet business, legal, and audit requirements; account data is deactivated when your relationship with Ethnix Group ends.

6.Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Because the App is provided in the context of an employment or business relationship, some records may need to be retained to comply with legal or contractual obligations. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

7.Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or internal company channels, and the effective date above will be revised accordingly.

9.Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, reach out through the contact form on our website:

Ethnix Group LLC, a Motion Logistics company
Contact: ethnixgroup.com — Get in touch

Ethnix Group Phone Verification · Last updated: September 22, 2026

See also: SMS Terms and Conditions

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how Motion Logistics, doing business as Ethnix Group ("Ethnix Group", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, retains, and protects information in connection with the Ethnix Group Phone Verification text messaging program (the "Program").

The Program has a single purpose: to send a one-time verification code to a customer's mobile phone so that we can confirm the phone number is correct and belongs to the customer. The Program does not send marketing, promotional, or recurring messages. This policy applies only to the Program and is separate from any other privacy policy published by Ethnix Group.

1.Information We Collect

When you take part in the Program, we collect only the following information:

  • Mobile phone number: the number you provide verbally to an Ethnix Group agent during the delivery process.
  • Consent record: a record that the consent notice was read to you and that you verbally agreed to receive the verification text message, including the agent who recorded it.
  • Verification timestamp: the date and time the verification code was sent and the date and time the number was confirmed.

We do not collect the content of any other messages on your device, your contacts, or your location through the Program.

2.How We Use This Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Send the one-time verification code to your mobile phone number;
  • Confirm that the mobile phone number you provided is correct and belongs to you;
  • Keep a record of your consent and of the verification, as required to comply with applicable law and carrier requirements;
  • Process opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.

We do not use this information to send marketing or promotional messages, and we do not use it for any purpose other than those listed above.

3.Sharing of Information

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your text messaging consent and opt-in data are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

To deliver verification messages, your mobile phone number and the message content are processed by the SMS service provider and wireless carriers that transmit the message. These providers act only on our behalf, only to deliver the message, and may not use your information for any other purpose. We may also disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or court order.

4.Data Retention

  • Verification codes are single-use and expire shortly after they are sent. They are not kept after they expire or are used.
  • Mobile phone number, consent record, and verification timestamp are kept only for as long as needed to confirm your phone number and to demonstrate that consent was obtained, as required by applicable law and carrier requirements. They are deleted once they are no longer needed for these purposes, or sooner if you request deletion (see Section 7).
  • Opt-out records are kept for as long as needed to make sure we do not send further messages to a number that has opted out.

5.Data Security

We protect Program information with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections, and access is limited to personnel and service providers who need it to operate the Program.

6.Opting Out

You can stop receiving text messages from the Program at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you reply STOP, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below. See our SMS Terms and Conditions for all supported keywords.

7.Requesting Deletion of Your Data

You may ask us to delete the mobile phone number and verification records associated with the Program by emailing info@ethnixgroup.com with the subject line "SMS data deletion request" or by calling (615) 986-2376. Please include the mobile phone number you want removed. We may contact you to confirm the request comes from the owner of that number.

We will respond within 30 days. If we must keep a limited record by law (for example, a record that the number has opted out so that we do not message it again), we will tell you and keep only what is required.

8.Children's Privacy

The Program is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through the Program.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.

10.Contact Us

If you have questions about this SMS Privacy Policy or the Program, contact us:
